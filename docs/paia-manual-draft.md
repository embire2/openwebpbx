## 7. Access procedure and remedies

Send a request to the Information Officer using the prescribed PAIA form where applicable. Clearly identify the record, preferred form of access and contact details; explain the right to exercise/protect and why the record is required when the private-body provisions require this. Provide authority to act for another person and proportionate proof of identity. Ask for assistance rather than sending passwords, connection codes or excessive identity documents.

The officer must acknowledge and process requests under the Act, assess third-party rights and lawful refusal grounds, communicate decisions and explain access arrangements, fees, exemptions and available remedies. Formal decisions are generally due within 30 days with only a permitted notified extension. Use the prescribed current fees/deposit rules and applicable exemptions, rather than an invented fixed fee. POPIA confirmation of whether personal information is held is free. POPIA correction/deletion/objection requests use their own procedures/forms.

Private bodies do not provide the public-body internal appeal route. A refusal must identify available Information Regulator complaint/court routes and applicable time limits. Verify the current official guide and particulars rather than requiring the requester to complete an invented appeal process.
