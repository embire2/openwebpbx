OpenWeb PBX

Android app demonstration

This earlier basic-workflow demonstration uses the actual native OpenWeb PBX 1.0.7 app and isolated demo accounts. It shows calling, voicemail and the ongoing connection notification. No customer information or outside calls are used. The 1.0.16 release includes transfers, second calls, three-way calling, availability controls, visible hold, personal Settings and readiness on office networks; see the current downloads and guide for those features.

Download the video

Native video calling is included in Android and Debian 1.0.16. Restarting the camera can briefly interrupt sound or pictures; see the video guide. The older demonstration above does not show its camera controls; a current video demonstration and final declarations are still needed for that store submission.

For Google Play review

The private App access instructions supplied through Play Console include a reusable login for the separate review portal. Reviewers can connect a phone, test an incoming call, use the audio test and try voicemail without accessing customer services.

The Google Play submission package is prepared. The app has not yet been submitted or approved.
