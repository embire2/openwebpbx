OpenWeb PBX

Android app demonstration

This earlier basic-workflow demonstration uses the actual native OpenWeb PBX 1.0.7 app and isolated demo accounts. It shows calling, voicemail and the ongoing connection notification. No customer information or outside calls are used. The current Android 1.0.19 release includes a native five-second logo launch screen that yields immediately to calls and updates, plus transfers, second calls, three-way calling, availability controls, visible hold and personal Settings. See the current downloads and guide for those features.

Download the video

Native video calling is included in Android and Debian 1.0.16. Restarting the camera can briefly interrupt sound or pictures; see the video guide. The older demonstration above does not show its camera controls; a current video demonstration and final declarations are still needed for that store submission.

The tenant-address checks introduced in Android 1.0.18 remain: each account uses its registered openwebpbx.com tenant address, and existing accounts confirm it once. This account change does not affect SIP desk phones. See the tenant-address guide.

For Google Play review

The private App access instructions supplied through Play Console include a reusable login for the separate review portal. Reviewers can connect a phone, test an incoming call, use the audio test and try voicemail without accessing customer services.

The signed Google Play 1.0.19 submission bundle is prepared. Store review and approval remain separate from this downloadable bundle.
