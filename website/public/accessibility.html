Accessibility

Report a barrier or request information in another format.

Updated 17 October 2026 · Open ISP cc

We aim to make the OpenWeb PBX public website usable with keyboard controls, screen readers, different screen sizes and light/dark appearance. Legal notices are available as HTML without an account or PDF download. The website remains usable when optional storage is rejected; core information and download links are available without JavaScript.

You can use Tab/Shift+Tab to move between controls and Enter/Space to activate buttons. Escape closes the privacy-settings dialog without accepting optional storage. Your browser's zoom and display settings remain available.

Get assistance

Contact Information Officer Leon Whitherall at hello@openwebpbx.com or 0873508601. Tell us which page/control caused difficulty, what you were trying to do and the format or assistance you need. You do not have to disclose a medical diagnosis.

Browser checks cover responsive layouts, focus and the privacy controls. They do not establish a complete independent accessibility audit or certification against every WCAG criterion. Report a problem so we can assess and improve it. This statement concerns the public website; the PBX administration and mobile app have separate interfaces.
