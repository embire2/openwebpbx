## How to maintain the checklist

- Keep IDs and existing acceptance counts stable until a goal's full outcome is met. **Build** means the complete equivalent still needs building, even when a foundation exists. **Partial** means some required workflow exists. **Verify** needs evidence through supported paths. **Needs input** identifies an external dependency.
- Check an item only when the stated outcome passes on applicable platforms with correct tenant permissions. Record exact source, release and test scope. Keep a failure and its later correction distinct. A successful registration, simulation or file validation does not certify physical call audio, carrier operation, Store approval or complete parity.
- New external inputs may be needed for real carrier/PMS/connector tests, device/Store qualification and unobserved backup features. Keep moving on isolated work while those inputs are pending. Keep credentials, customer backups, test endpoint details and private evidence out of Git.
- See [working guide](AGENTS.md), [feature coverage](docs/feature-coverage.md) and [platform release policy](docs/independent-platform-releases.md) for working rules and detailed scope. The checklist preserves earlier evidence under its original version where it is still useful.
